  </h2>

  <p className="text-gray-700 mb-6 leading-relaxed">
    Maharashtra uses a percentile-based ranking system rather than raw marks. This is crucial to understand because the exam is conducted over multiple shifts with varying difficulty levels. A normalization process ensures absolute fairness across all sessions.
  </p>

  <div className="bg-orange-50 border-l-4 border-orange-500 p-6 mb-8 rounded">
    <p className="text-gray-800 mb-3">
      <strong>What Your Percentile Means:</strong>
    </p>
    <p className="text-gray-700">
      A percentile of 95 means 95% of candidates scored equal to or below you. It is NOT a percentage of marks. For example, scoring 90% of marks might translate to 99.5 percentile if others scored lower on that particular shift.
    </p>
  </div>
