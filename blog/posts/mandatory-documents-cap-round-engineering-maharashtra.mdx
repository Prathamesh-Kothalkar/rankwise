  <h2 className="text-2xl font-bold mt-12 mb-4 text-gray-900">
    Core Documents: The Non-Negotiable Essentials
  </h2>

  <h3 className="text-xl font-semibold mt-8 mb-3 text-gray-800">
    1. Provisional Seat Allotment Letter (PSAL)
  </h3>

  <p className="text-gray-700 mb-4">
    This is your golden ticket—the official letter confirming your seat before the scrutiny process begins.
  </p>

  <ul className="list-disc list-inside text-gray-700 mb-6 space-y-2">
    <li><strong>What it contains:</strong> Your application number, all-India rank, merit list position, allotted college, branch, category, and round number</li>
    <li><strong>Where to get it:</strong> Download from the official DTE Maharashtra website or receive via registered email</li>
    <li><strong>Required copies:</strong> Minimum 3 originals; scrutiny center, college admission office, and your personal records</li>
    <li><strong>Critical detail:</strong> Print only on the prescribed dates—printing it early or late can raise authenticity questions</li>
  </ul>

  <h3 className="text-xl font-semibold mt-8 mb-3 text-gray-800">
    2. Caste Validity Certificate
  </h3>

  <p className="text-gray-700 mb-4">
    If you've applied under SC, ST, OBC, or EBC category, this certificate is absolutely mandatory. Many students mistakenly believe their caste listed in the MHT CET form is sufficient—it isn't.
  </p>

  <ul className="list-disc list-inside text-gray-700 mb-6 space-y-2">
    <li><strong>Authority:</strong> Must be issued by the Collector's office of your district (or designated talathi/patwari for Gram Panchayat level)</li>
    <li><strong>Validity:</strong> Generally valid for 5 years from issue date; check your state's current policy</li>
    <li><strong>Format requirement:</strong> Must specifically mention your caste falls under SC/ST/OBC/EBC category as per Maharashtra state list</li>
    <li><strong>Common rejection reason:</strong> Certificate stating caste name only, without explicit category validation</li>
    <li><strong>Processing time:</strong> Allow 15-20 days; apply immediately if you don't already have this</li>
  </ul>

  <h3 className="text-xl font-semibold mt-8 mb-3 text-gray-800">
    3. Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) Certificate
  </h3>

  <p className="text-gray-700 mb-4">
    Required exclusively for OBC candidates. This certificate proves your family's income doesn't exceed the creamy layer threshold, making you eligible for reserved seats.
  </p>

  <ul className="list-disc list-inside text-gray-700 mb-6 space-y-2">
    <li><strong>Income limit (2024-25):</strong> Annual family income should not exceed ₹8 lakhs</li>
    <li><strong>Authority:</strong> Collector's office or Circle Officer (in rural areas, through Gram Panchayat)</li>
    <li><strong>Validity:</strong> Valid for one financial year; if issued in 2025, typically valid through March 2026</li>
    <li><strong>Income proof required:</strong> Submit income certificate, IT returns, or Form 16 while applying</li>
    <li><strong>What happens if you exceed limit:</strong> You remain OBC, but lose reserved seat eligibility—this is critical to understand before CAP registration</li>
  </ul>

  <h3 className="text-xl font-semibold mt-8 mb-3 text-gray-800">
    4. Income Certificate
  </h3>

  <p className="text-gray-700 mb-4">
    Distinct from the NCL certificate, this document verifies your family's annual income. Required for all candidates applying for TFWS (Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme) or means-tested scholarships.
  </p>

  <ul className="list-disc list-inside text-gray-700 mb-6 space-y-2">
    <li><strong>Who must submit:</strong> All candidates applying for TFWS; general category students can opt to provide it for financial aid eligibility</li>
    <li><strong>Authority:</strong> Talathi (Gram Panchayat), Sarpanch, or Collector's office depending on rural/urban classification</li>
    <li><strong>Income brackets (TFWS eligibility):</strong> Annual family income ≤ ₹4.5 lakhs for Maharashtra</li>
    <li><strong>Required along with:</strong> Latest income tax returns, Form 16, or salary certificate from employer</li>
    <li><strong>Date requirement:</strong> Should be issued within the current or preceding financial year</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 className="text-2xl font-bold mt-12 mb-4 text-gray-900">
    Supporting Documents: Don't Show Up Without These
  </h2>

  <h3 className="text-xl font-semibold mt-8 mb-3 text-gray-800">
    5. 10th and 12th Mark Sheets
  </h3>

  <ul className="list-disc list-inside text-gray-700 mb-6 space-y-2">
    <li>Original mark sheets with school seal</li>
    <li>At least 2 attested copies each</li>
    <li>If originals are unavailable, submit duplicate certificate from school principal</li>
  </ul>

  <h3 className="text-xl font-semibold mt-8 mb-3 text-gray-800">
    6. MHT CET Admit Card and Score Card
  </h3>

  <ul className="list-disc list-inside text-gray-700 mb-6 space-y-2">
    <li>Both original and 2 copies</li>
    <li>Digital copies also recommended (PDF format)</li>
  </ul>

  <h3 className="text-xl font-semibold mt-8 mb-3 text-gray-800">
    7. Proof of Nationality and Residency
  </h3>

  <ul className="list-disc list-inside text-gray-700 mb-6 space-y-2">
    <li>Birth certificate (original + 2 copies) OR</li>
    <li>Aadhar card (2 copies) OR</li>
    <li>Passport (2 copies)</li>
    <li><strong>Maharashtra residency:</strong> Provide domicile certificate or residential proof from talathi</li>
  </ul>

  <h3 className="text-xl font-semibold mt-8 mb-3 text-gray-800">
    8. Character Certificate
  </h3>

  <ul className="list-disc list-inside text-gray-700 mb-6 space-y-2">
    <li>Issued by your 12th standard school principal</li>
    <li>On official school letterhead</li>
    <li>Date should be recent (within 3 months of CAP verification)</li>
  </ul>

  <h3 className="text-xl font-semibold mt-8 mb-3 text-gray-800">
    9. Migration Certificate (if applicable)
  </h3>

  <ul className="list-disc list-inside text-gray-700 mb-6 space-y-2">
    <li>Required if your 10th and 12th from different schools</li>
    <li>Or if you changed schools during senior secondary</li>
    <li>Obtained from your previous school's principal</li>
  </ul>

  <h3 className="text-xl font-semibold mt-8 mb-3 text-gray-800">
    10. Medical Fitness Certificate
  </h3>

  <ul className="list-disc list-inside text-gray-700 mb-6 space-y-2">
    <li>Prescribed format from DTE Maharashtra</li>
    <li>Issued by registered medical practitioner</li>
    <li>Valid for 6 months; renew if expired before CAP completion</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 className="text-2xl font-bold mt-12 mb-4 text-gray-900">
    The Document Submission Timeline: When to Do What
  </h2>

  <h3 className="text-xl font-semibold mt-8 mb-3 text-gray-800">
    3-4 Weeks Before Your Allotment
  </h3>

  <ul className="list-disc list-inside text-gray-700 mb-6 space-y-2">
    <li>Apply for caste validity certificate (if needed)</li>
    <li>Apply for non-creamy layer certificate (OBC candidates)</li>
    <li>Gather income certificate application</li>
    <li>Request character certificate from school</li>
  </ul>

  <h3 className="text-xl font-semibold mt-8 mb-3 text-gray-800">
    1 Week After Allotment
  </h3>

  <ul className="list-disc list-inside text-gray-700 mb-6 space-y-2">
    <li>Download and print PSAL (Provisional Seat Allotment Letter)</li>
    <li>Get medical fitness certificate done</li>
    <li>Collect all certificates from issuing authorities</li>
    <li>Get all documents photocopied and attested</li>
  </ul>

  <h3 className="text-xl font-semibold mt-8 mb-3 text-gray-800">
    Before Scrutiny Center Visit
  </h3>

  <ul className="list-disc list-inside text-gray-700 mb-6 space-y-2">
    <li>Cross-check every document against the official checklist</li>
    <li>Verify all certificates are original and properly sealed</li>
    <li>Ensure no certificate has expired validity</li>
    <li>Prepare a folder with documents in the prescribed order</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 className="text-2xl font-bold mt-12 mb-4 text-gray-900">
    Common Rejection Patterns: Learn From Others' Mistakes
  </h2>

  <p className="text-gray-700 mb-4">
    Based on scrutiny center feedback, here are the documents most frequently cited in rejection notices:
  </p>

  <ul className="list-disc list-inside text-gray-700 mb-6 space-y-3">
    <li><strong>Expired caste certificate:</strong> "Certificate validity period has lapsed." Solution: Apply for new certificate immediately if yours is older than 5 years.</li>
    <li><strong>Missing NCL certificate:</strong> "OBC candidate without non-creamy layer validation." Solution: Don't assume your MHT CET registration covers this; get a fresh certificate.</li>
    <li><strong>Income certificate mismatch:</strong> "Family income exceeds TFWS limit as per attached income certificate." Solution: Verify the exact income limit for your application year and ensure documentation supports it.</li>
    <li><strong>Unsigned or missing official seal:</strong> "Character certificate lacks issuing authority's signature." Solution: Always verify seal placement and authorized signatory before submission.</li>
    <li><strong>Photocopies instead of originals:</strong> "Supporting documents not in original form." Solution: Bring originals to scrutiny centers; photocopies are backup only.</li>
  </ul>
